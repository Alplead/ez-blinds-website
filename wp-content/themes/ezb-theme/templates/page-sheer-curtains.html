What matters most

Filtered daylight

Think about how much brightness you want to keep while reducing the harshness of direct light.

Privacy layering

Sheers often need to be considered together with a separate privacy or blockout layer for night use.

Stacking and fall

Track position, stacking space and how the fabric finishes at the floor all change the final look.

What we check before confirming the design

  • window and wall width
  • track position and available stacking space
  • floor level and desired finished drop
  • how much privacy the room needs at different times of day
  • whether another layer needs to sit behind the sheer

Fabric range, track system, colour, lead time and care instructions will be published only after current supplier details are verified.
