Start with what the room needs

Privacy

Think about whether the room needs daytime screening, stronger night privacy or both.

Light

Consider glare, filtered daylight and how dark you want the room to become when the blind is down.

Operation

Window width, access, furniture placement and how often the blind is used can influence the most practical operating setup.

What we measure and check

  • window width, drop and available fixing space
  • where the blind can sit relative to handles and trims
  • how much light gap is acceptable for the room
  • whether adjacent windows should line up visually
  • how the blind will be reached and operated

Fabric ranges, colours, motor options, component origin and warranty wording will be added only after the current supplier details are verified.
