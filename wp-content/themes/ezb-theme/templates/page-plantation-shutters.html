What matters most

Panel layout

The number of panels and where they hinge or meet can affect both the appearance and how easily the window is accessed.

Light and privacy

Adjustable louvres let you balance privacy with daylight, but the final result still depends on the window and room orientation.

Room conditions

Moisture, heat, opening size and how frequently the window is used all matter when selecting the appropriate current product range.

What we check at the window

  • window shape, recess depth and fixing surfaces
  • handles, locks and other hardware that may interfere
  • how panels need to open for cleaning or window access
  • visual alignment across adjacent windows
  • room conditions that may affect material choice
