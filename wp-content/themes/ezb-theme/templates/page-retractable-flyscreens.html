Where it can work well

Sliding openings

Useful where a conventional hinged screen would interfere with the opening or circulation space.

Wide openings

Suitable configurations can be considered for wider openings after an on-site measure confirms the details.

Indoor–outdoor living

Helpful where the view and connection to an outdoor area matter as much as practical screening.

What we check before recommending a system

  • opening width and height
  • door operation and available fixing surfaces
  • track position and threshold constraints
  • how often the opening is used
  • whether the screen needs to disappear visually when not in use

Exact system, mesh, hardware, colour, warranty and manufacturing claims remain subject to current supplier verification and the site measure.
