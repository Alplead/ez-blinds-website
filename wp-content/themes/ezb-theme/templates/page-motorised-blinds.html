When motorisation can make sense

Access

High, wide or awkwardly positioned blinds can be difficult to reach manually.

Routine

Blinds used repeatedly every day may benefit from simpler or grouped operation.

Control

Control method, charging or power and compatible blind size all need to be considered together.

What we need to confirm

  • blind type and dimensions
  • available power or charging access
  • how many blinds need to be controlled
  • whether local, grouped or connected control is appropriate
  • current compatible motor and control range

Motor brands, minimum widths, battery specifications, bridges, remotes and warranty details remain subject to current supplier verification.
