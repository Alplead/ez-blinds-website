## Role of each content family

### Products
Conversion-oriented pages for active products. Each page explains suitability, practical choices, measurement considerations, real media, FAQs, related projects, one related guide and a clear quote path.

### Projects
First-party installation proof. Project pages should use genuine EZ imagery and only grounded project facts. Do not combine unrelated homes into one case study.

### Advice
Evergreen decision-support / pillar content that should remain useful for a long period. Current examples:
- Privacy vs Daylight
- Retractable Flyscreen Suitability
- When Motorisation Makes Sense

### Blog
Ongoing topical content for practical questions, comparisons, seasonal issues, local Melbourne context and deeper product education. Blog articles should support Product or Advice pages rather than duplicate them.

Initial development-only article candidates:
- Blockout or sunscreen roller blinds: where should you start?
- Sheer curtains, privacy and layering: what changes from day to night?
- Plantation shutters: what should be checked before you choose them?
- Is your doorway suitable for a retractable flyscreen?
- When does motorisation make sense for blinds?

### Service Areas
Grounded service-area information. Avoid thin suburb-page multiplication.

## Internal-linking principle

Product -> related Blog guide -> relevant Product / Contact
Advice -> relevant Product / Project
Project -> relevant Product / Contact
Blog -> relevant Product / Advice / Contact

Every content family should lead toward a useful next step without forcing all traffic to the Home page.
